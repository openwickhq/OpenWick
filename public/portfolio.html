Portfolio

Drop your Zerodha holdings file here or · CSV or Excel (.xlsx)

Where to get the file

  1. Kite (web): Holdings → the download icon above the list → CSV.
  2. Console: Portfolio → Holdings → Download (Excel works too).
  3. Console tradebook: Reports → Tradebook → Equity → pick dates from your first trade → Download CSV. Holdings are then worked out from your trades, with realised profit.

The file is read in your browser. Only the stock symbols, quantities and average prices are kept, in data/portfolio.json on this computer. Nothing is sent to Zerodha or anywhere else.
