# Code of conduct

OpenWick follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful and patient, welcome newcomers, criticise ideas and not people, and assume good intent. Harassment, insults, personal attacks and sharing other people's private information are not accepted.

**Reporting.** If something happens, report the comment or user with GitHub's "Report content" option (the `...` menu on any comment), or open a private report through the repository's Security tab. The maintainers will review every report and keep the reporter's identity private. Maintainers may edit or remove comments, and block people who break these rules.
